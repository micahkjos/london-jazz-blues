The Scene

London after dark

London's jazz runs from Ronnie Scott's, a Soho basement since 1959, and the 606 in Chelsea to small rooms like the Vortex in Dalston. Blues and soul nights fill the Blues Kitchen and the Jazz Cafe in Camden. A younger generation, many of them trained by Tomorrow's Warriors, mixes jazz with Afrobeat, grime and Caribbean sounds, and in 2023 Ezra Collective became the first jazz act to win the Mercury Prize.

The words matter too. Each venue decides what counts as "jazz," "blues," "soul" or "tribute," and those labels shape whose music gets the big rooms. Below is every gig this week, in the order it happens and in each venue's own words.
